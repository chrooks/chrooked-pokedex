# Design Log

The **why** behind each makeover — the ledger records what changed; this records the
direction picked, options rejected, and corrections given along the way. Appended by
`/makeover` at the commit stage. Raw material for refining the suggest-skill rubrics
(the pacing anchors were born from corrections like these) and for loop-audits.

---

## 2026-07-23 — Galvantula line

- **Direction:** trapper role, leaned in hard ("I love the trapper role lets lean into that").
- **New mechanics:** Web Weaver ability — created explicitly reusable for other mons; includes arena-trap effect. Electroweb buffed with high paralysis chance.
- **Corrections:** keep Sticky Web on the learnset so alternate abilities still work; STAB progressions dictated (Thunder Shock → Shock Wave → Electroweb → Discharge → Thunder; Struggle Bug → Infestation → Signal Beam → Bug Buzz); poison coverage + thematic status moves + Leech Life; Compound Eyes → Carnivore.

## 2026-07-23 — Zoroark line

- **Direction:** open-ended → lore research first; landed on broad coverage ("feels like the kind of pokemon that would").
- **New mechanics:** Night Daze buffed (underpowered signature move — now 40% accuracy drop, 10 PP) and made the L0 on-evo reward over Night Slash.
- **Corrections:** after the buff, Night Daze moved late in the level-up curve; coverage chosen for role battle-wise AND lore-wise (Fiery Dance, Sludge Bomb, Grass Knot, Focus Blast).
